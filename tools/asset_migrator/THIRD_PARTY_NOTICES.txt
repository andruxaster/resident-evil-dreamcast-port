Archivo Black and Archivo Narrow (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
============================================================

core/HdFonts.cpp carries two fonts by Omnibus-Type, unmodified, and writes them
into the HD folder, where the game draws its HD text in them:

  Archivo Black, version 1.006 (fonts/ArchivoBlack-Regular.ttf, embedded
  through core/HdFontTitle.inc) -> HD/<USA|JPN>/Data/TITLEFNT.TTF
  Archivo Narrow SemiBold, version 3.002 (fonts/ArchivoNarrow-SemiBold.ttf,
  embedded through core/HdFontText.inc) -> HD/USA/Data/FONTUS.TTF and
  HD/JPN/Data/FONT.TTF

Each font file carries its own copyright and licence notice in its name table.
Their licences follow, each as its project ships it (fonts/ArchivoBlack-OFL.txt,

Libertinus Serif and M PLUS 2 (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
=========================================================

core/HdFonts.cpp carries two more fonts and writes them into the HD folder,
where the game draws its 8x14 text in them. Both are Modified Versions under
the licence: subsets made by tools/gen_hd_fonts.py with fontTools. Neither
licence declares a Reserved Font Name.

  Libertinus Serif SemiBold, version 7.051, subset to Latin-1 and its
  punctuation (fonts/LibertinusSerif-SemiBold-Subset.ttf, embedded through
  core/HdFontText14.inc) -> HD/USA/Data/FONTUS_14.TTF
  M PLUS 2, instanced at SemiBold (wght 600) and subset to the
  characters of the Japanese font sheet (fonts/MPLUS2-SemiBold-Subset.ttf,
  embedded through core/HdFontTextJpn14.inc) -> HD/JPN/Data/FONT_14.TTF

Each font file keeps its copyright and licence notice in its name table. Their
licences follow as their projects ship them (fonts/LibertinusSerif-OFL.txt,
fonts/MPLUS2-OFL.txt).
